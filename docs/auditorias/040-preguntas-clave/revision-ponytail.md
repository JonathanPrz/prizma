# ponytail-review · Phase B

What this change does: This change adds the six questions and conditions required by phase B. It saves each person's modality, protocol response, success criterion and Work sharing decision, updates suggestions and document text, and keeps prior choices available for review.

Looks good. Ship.

Checked the full diff and connected code: both person normalizers, normalizarDatos and backup validation, agreement validation and status, case switching, field listeners and privacy, access/practice/Work filters, the three card builders, safety links, selection and notes, document inclusion, the centre's sharing gate, HTML preview and pdfmake output. Read the updated task, seed JSON and current AGENTS.md. Assumed one local user, up to ten cases in each domain and the current 240-card catalog.

New fields are bounded and normalized at storage/import boundaries. Unknown modalities fall back to No aplica; protocol requires a real boolean; criterion is capped at 200 characters and also validated before agreement completion. A previously complete agreement without a criterion becomes Por revisar without losing its roles, dates, choices or notes. Existing complete-agreement fixtures now provide a criterion; the new test separately covers the legacy transition.

The exclusion is explicit and narrow. Mixed-format cards retain compatible alternatives through shared text helpers used by cards, selection and documents. A changed modality does not silently delete a selected card or note: the old choice remains removable in the backpack, agreement completion is blocked and a draft document identifies the mismatch. During review, the incompatibility check was limited to applicable cards so changing education level cannot leave an invisible old choice blocking the agreement. A regression preserves that old note and checks the new level. The content decision in the exclusion list still needs Claude's normal PR review; this engineering review does not claim that clinical/content approval happened.

Safety links point to one existing card, open its collapsed ancestors and move focus without selecting it. The alert covers the specified conditions and adds the explicit safety options when practice or Work makes them relevant. The criterion, protocol and sharing text reach the shared document model and both renderers. The centre does not copy a personal criterion or note automatically; it gets a blank criterion line, preserving the existing promise to share only reviewed centre adjustments.
